Kiribot Privacy Policy

Draft. This policy is not yet in effect.

1. Who is responsible

Kiriaka is the data controller for Kiribot, the Discord bot and Lineup planner Activity used by the Kiriaka music association. Contact: dev@kiriaka.se.

2. What we collect

  • Discord details: user ID, username, server nickname, display name, server membership and roles.
  • Member profile: active or inactive status and details you choose to enter: dietary needs, driving licence, car access and key holding.
  • Events: your signups, responses, notes and instrument section, and your place in concert lineups.
  • Messages to the bot: contact forms you submit, which are posted in Kiriaka Discord threads, and lineup images you ask the bot to send you by DM.
  • Activity logs: what members do in the bot, including names, plus error information.
  • Sign-in data: a Discord access token used to confirm your identity and role. The planner holds it in memory while open and does not store it permanently.
  • Web session: the Kiriaka year wheel site uses a kiribot_session cookie to keep you signed in.

Dietary needs can sometimes reveal health or religious information. We process them only with your explicit consent. Entering them is optional, so enter only what Kiriaka needs to plan food. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing them or contacting us.

3. Why we use it

We use this data to run Kiriaka's activities: managing member details, signups, lineups, workgroups, calendars and reminders, and keeping the service secure and working. We also keep a historical record of events to support planning and documentation of Kiriaka's recurring concerts.

Our legal basis is legitimate interests: running the association's activities and keeping the service secure. Dietary details are the exception and are based on your explicit consent. Details you volunteer are optional unless Kiriaka tells you otherwise.

4. Who can see it

Kiriaka members may see relevant details through the bot, such as signups, lineups and lists. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

These service providers process data for us:

  • Discord: the platform, sign-in and messages.
  • Google: Sheets and Drive for event data, and Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the planner loads.
  • Cloudflare: hosting for the planner and network connection to the bot.
  • Cybrancee: hosting for the bot and its data.

Cybrancee hosts the bot in the EU/EEA. Discord, Google and Cloudflare may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, under their own data transfer terms.

5. How long we keep it

  • Activity logs are kept for about three months, then deleted.
  • Access-token checks are cached in server memory for about one minute.
  • Member profile details are deleted when you leave Kiriaka.
  • Signup notes are deleted 30 days after the event is archived. Events are normally archived shortly after they take place.
  • Other event data, including signups, responses and lineups, is kept indefinitely as a historical record of Kiriaka's recurring concerts. You can object to this at any time.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask for access to your data, and ask us to correct, delete, restrict or transfer it. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests. To make a request, contact dev@kiriaka.se. You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

7. Security

Planner data is available only to signed-in members with the required Kiriaka role, and secrets are kept off the public site. No system is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and tell members about significant changes.
